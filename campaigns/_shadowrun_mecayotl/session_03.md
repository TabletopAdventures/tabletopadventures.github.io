# New Job - Ares’ Public Humiliation
> Wednesday, February 6, 2086

**Bull**, one of the squad's fixers, sent out a blast:

> Meet at *Yttrium* in 6 hours, new work.

*Yttrium* is a Matrix Nightclub known for it's "Neon Nebula" aesthetic and discrete rooms. They met a different **Mr. Johnson** that was super interested in sticking it to *Ares Macrotechnology*.

**Mr. Johnson**, with a flair for the dramatic, wants to turn the upcoming *Ares Executive Lancer* debut into a high-profile corporate disaster. Instead of messy extractions or wetwork, he demands a creative, non-lethal public humiliation for the autonomous security rig during *Ares'* global live-streamed launch ahead of the *International Auto Show*.

The team is tasked with hijacking the Lancer’s systems, turning the showcase into an embarrassing "performance art" piece that trashes the *Ares* brand’s reputation. Whether it’s forcing a safety dance or leaking the CEO’s dirty laundry, the goal is a legendary tech-fail that leaves *Ares* looking incompetent. The runners negotiated a base of ¥8,000 each, with a fat performance bonus on the line if they really twist the knife in *Ares's* back. He wants a spectacular, front-page face-plant, and he’s paying well to make sure the spectacle is unforgettable.


## Investigation
The crew started pounding the pavement, hitting up contacts and burning through Matrix nodes.

- **Bigoonia** worked the street and found there is some specialized hardware that could bridge Sleeper and **NEEV** into the vehicle’s guts.
- **NEEV** leaned on her contacts and caught wind that *Ares's* next earnings report is a sinking ship. Another lead pinned down where the Lancer might be stashed.
- **Neon Ghost** used his background to confirm that any dirt they drop before the auto show will gut *Ares's* market share. He went boots-on-the-ground to scout, flagging a magical barrier inside the facility.
- **Toolbox** hit the Matrix, pulling up the location: a reinforced manor.
- **Sleeper** scrubbed the same nets, but didn't find anything beyond what **Toolbox** already dug up.


## Scouting Plan

After comparing notes, the crew decided to get eyes on the target to confirm the Lancer was actually in the nest.

**Bigoonia**, **Neon Ghost**, **Getafix**, and **Toolbox** are going to breach the facility, while **Sleeper** and **NEEV** provide support and electronic overwatch.
