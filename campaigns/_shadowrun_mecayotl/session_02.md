# Finalizing Plans
> Monday, January 13, 2086

**NEEV** dropped a fragging weird idea on the table, and whether it was brilliance or pure sludge is still up for debate. The plan: geek the SINner with a hacked whip. Then **Toolbox** piped up, suggesting we wire the whole thing with boom-stuff to make sure the job stayed finished. After some heavy back-and-forth, the crew decided to nix the high-profile pyrotechnics and keep the noise down.

They decided to ambush **Gwendoline** at the gym. They knew she was a creature of habit, showing up daily, and it was the perfect spot to geek her while she was stripped of her hardware and eyes-off. The plan: zero the camera grid and slot her while she was in the showers.

# The Setup
> Tuesday, January 14, 2086

**Neon Ghost** hit the gym early to run surveillance, while **Getafix**, **Bigoonia**, and **Toolbox** went hunting for a back-door entrance. **Gwendoline** waltzed in with another Ork, hitting the floor for her routine. 

The alley team got lucky. **Getafix** had his lockpick kit so they were able to sneak into a storage room. **Neon Ghost** sent word that **Gwendoline** was on the move. The Ork she walked in with wasn't pumping iron, he was clearly there to scope out the gym goers. Meanwhile, **NEEV** and **Sleeper** were deep in the basement of **Sleeper**’s mom, hard-lining the electronic sabotage. All the pieces were set.

# Heating up the Shower Room
**Neon Ghost** tracked **Gwendoline** to the locker room, grabbed his gear, and dipped, signaling the team that the mark was headed for the showers. He staked out the entrance. **Getafix** couldn't crack the shower room lock, so he popped the locker room door instead. **Sleeper** flatlined the camera feed, permanently. **Toolbox** took point, holding a "Wet Floor" sign as a pathetic cover, but as **Bigoonia** rounded the corner, **Sledge** went white-hot, drawing iron on **Toolbox**. **Bigoonia** charged, and **Sledge** opened up. One absolute fragging nightmare shot to the face later, and **Toolbox** was clinging to what was left of his face. **Sledge** then ventilated **Bigoonia** before she could close the distance. **Neon Ghost** pulled some vocal trid-trickery, echoing his favorite anime character, screaming "He's got a gun!" before bolting to secure the alley exit.

**Bigoonia**, **Toolbox**, and **Getafix** hammered **Sledge** with lead and gel, while **Gwendoline** tried to scramble. She tried to breach the door but was flailing. **Sledge** squeezed off another round at **Bigoonia**, then **Getafix** peppered him with gel rounds. **Bigoonia** closed the gap and paid **Sledge** back with a face-full of shock glove, dropping him like a sack of bricks.

**Gwendoline** realized she was trapped, so she turned to scrap whoever took out her muscle. She shanked **Bigoonia** with a blade she’d stashed in the showers, but **Bigoonia** swung back, knocking the wind right out of her. **Getafix** unloaded another gel blast, dropping **Gwendoline** to the cold floor. **Bigoonia** ripped her gloves off and hammered the unconscious **Gwendoline** like she was making a bowl of mashed potatoes.

# Aftermath
**Toolbox** was looking like a piece of Swiss cheese, and **Bigoonia** was bleeding out too. They grabbed the wounded, burned rubber through the back exit, and disappeared into the sprawl before the blue-and-whites could swarm the scene.
