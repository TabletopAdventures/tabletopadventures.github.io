# Roundup
> Friday, February 16, 2086

The second *Maria* shattered the sprinkler to summon the cavalry, **Sleeper** was already dancing through the local grid. With a flick of mental code, he strangled the building's automated distress signals in the crib, ensuring Lonestar and EMS remained completely deaf to the chaos.

# Extraction and Evac

Panicked denizens fled into the rainy courtyard, driven by the echo of gunshots and the blaring of the fire alarm triggered by the busted sprinklers. Up in the Matrix, **Sleeper** ran interference, intercepting the automated alerts and spoofing the dispatch signals so Lonestar and EMS wouldn't ruin the party. 

With the building in full meltdown, **Wyrm** channeled the mana and cast *Invisibility* over the subdued family members, **Neon Ghost**, and **Toolbox**. Serving as the invisible ferryman, **Neon Ghost** began extracting the targets, moving *Maria* and *Valentina* down to the waiting stepvan where **Toolbox** held down the fort while **Sleeper** snacked.

You never know what you're going to encounter in the Sixth World. *Maria* was carrying a concealed blade, which **Toolbox** quickly confiscated. Unwilling to go quietly into corporate servitude, she snapped the metal restraints, forcing **Toolbox** to give her a good jolt with his shock gloves. The harsh reality of the shadows set in, though it was briefly softened when **Toolbox** handed the terrified little girl some stickers, offering a gruff apology for knocking out her grandmother right in front of her. 

As the runners finalized the loading process, things took a surreal turn. In the midst of the fleeing crowds, **Toolbox** caught a glimpse of something impossible, a dragon, moving amidst the metahuman panic.

Over the comms, **Wyrm** confirmed the madness, having heard the unmistakable, resonant sound of the creature. But there was no time to stick around and investigate myths. They punched the gas.

# Handoff

The dropoff at the cold, sterile *Wintek Processing* facility went according to plan, though the runners caught whispers from the corporate suits that the *Quezada* family was being fast-tracked to an undisclosed black site. Best not to ask questions.

**Wyrm** suddenly ducked, head tilted toward the smog-choked sky. He was absolutely convinced he heard it: a heavy, rhythmic pounding of air, sounding exactly like what he imagined the flapping of dragon wings would be. Not a single other soul seemed to notice. Was the sprawl finally frying his meat-brain, or did a literal fragging dragon just cruise the haze overhead?

# Payday
> Sunday, February 24, 2086

They rendezvoused with **Mr. Johnson** to finalize the contract. Professional to the end, they handed over the data from the trackers they had planted on the family prior to the breach, collected their hard-earned *¥78,000*, and faded back into the shadows.

Three and a half months of downtime followed. The crew laid low and spent their creds and karma. **NEEV** vanished into the Resonance, dedicating time to complex Sprite acquisitions, while **Neon Ghost** took a different approach to relaxation—hitting up a string of anime conventions and browsing mall ninja shops for new blades. It wasn't all fun and games for **Neon Ghost** though, he earned Initiate Rank 2.

Meanwhile, **Sleeper** couldn't leave well enough alone. Digging into the matrix trails, he checked the status of the trackers. Only one was still broadcasting: the *Alejandro's*. The signal was pinging from a corporate morgue. Too many possibilities to make a guess beyond they're pretty sure he's dead. **Sleeper** closed the connection and kept the grim intel to himself.

# Work Dried Up
> Thursday, May 30, 2086

After months of flatlined biz, the commlinks were finally buzzing again. **Aleksy** let everyone know the **Mr. Johnson** from their last job wanted to meet at noon on June 3rd at *Four Floors*.

# Dr. Who?
> Monday, June 3, 2086

The job was a classic corporate extraction: "collect" a high-value scientist from a rival AAA megacorp and "re-allocate" them to the client's payroll. The runners aren't sure if *Dr. Aris Thorne* will come willingly or not. The runners negotiated a cool *¥54,000* to capture this one asset, how hard can it be?

During the legwork phase, **Sleeper** dredged up a datatrail of older public records on the mark, mapping out their history, while the meat team hit the sprawl to recon the corp's housing enclave. What they found wasn't wiz. The residential compound was a fragging fortress. The target and their colleagues only arrived and departed via heavily armed, escorted convoys.

No matter the vector, this is looking like a serious hoop-ache.
