# R&R? Nope
> Saturday, February 16, 2086

Less than a day after finishing the last job **Aleksy** lit up comms with another job from **Mr. Johnson** and to meet at *Four Floors* on Friday.

# Should Have Paid
> Friday, February 16, 2086

**Mr. Johnson** wasted no time describing the need for a collection run. The *Quezada* family made the fatal mistake of taking out a debt and are unable to pay. Contractually they are now required to work for the company, under strict supervision, corp-speak for indentured servitude. The job is simple on paper: collect the family members, and bag a fat cred-stick bonus if you can grab them all by March 15th.

It’s standard operating procedure in the Sixth World. Megacorporations hold extraterritoriality, making them judge, jury, and sovereign nation. When you ink a contract with them, your life is the collateral—default, and they will collect. The runners were just the repo men hired to finalize the paperwork, striking an agreement with **Mr. Johnson** for *¥6,500* a head, plus a *¥26,000* bonus for bagging the whole set. The dropoff for this corporate reclamation? A cold, sterile *Wintek Processing* facility.

# Investigation
They were told where the family lived and immediately staked out the rooftop apartment complex. Running smooth in the shadows, **Wyrm** used invisibility on **Neon Ghost** and then they slipped in and planted trackers on each family member, slaving the feeds to **Sleeper** as the owner of the trackers. Between shifts watching the marks, the crew saw cyber tacos were for sale in the courtyard and had to check them out. They put edible circuits and glowing soy-stuff on the shells/tortillas, real street-gourmet drek.

Jacked in and riding the Matrix grid, **Sleeper** started to dive into what could be found online and identified that *Alejandro* has a failing liver issue. The catch? He needs to remain alcohol free to be eligible for a liver transplant. They trawled the datatrails and looked for any other weakness or strengths the family members had, but nothing really came up in the wash.

Back in the physical world, the meat team stayed at the apartment complex for a while, quietly observing the family members living out their doomed routines. Turns out, *Alejandro* was sneaking booze and burning his transplant chances, while *Isabella* grinds away because she works at the local BTL lounge. *Roberto* enjoys throwing away his scrip spending time in the Joy bar. The muscle of the family, *Carlos* and *Diego* head to the gym with some sort of regularity, and they have grueling blue-collar jobs that last until late. Meanwhile, *Sofia*, *Maria*, and *Valentina* went out for some fresh air, unaware the shadows were watching. While observing, **Toolbox** tried to get fresh with the locals but only got a cold shoulder.

# Easy Way or the Hard Way?

The streetlights flickered as the family members began returning; the runners debated rushing in vs waiting it out. In a rare show of restraint, they decided to go for it once everyone was home, and to actually try diplomacy. Reaching into the depths of magic, **Wyrm** used *Clairvoyance* and saw everyone was locked inside. **NEEV** began talking to them through the barrier, but the velvet glove approach failed; reactions inside ranged from blind panick to grabbing a gun. Time for the iron fist. The mag-lock was easily bypassed and **Neon Ghost** flew inside like a wraith, crashing his teloscoping staff onto *Carlos* who had foolishly gone for a bat. Continuing to peer into the astral, **Wyrm** caught that *Roberto* pulled out a heavy rifle and quickly warned the crew over the commlink.

Chaos erupted in the cramped hab-unit. *Sofia*, *Diego*, and *Valentina* were hiding, *Sofia* taking up residence in the bathroom, *Diego* cowering on the other side of his flimsy door, and *Valentina* curled up under the desk. But *Maria* had street-smarts. She knew she couldn't stand up to a full group of hardened runners, so she grabbed a pan from dinner and busted the head off the sprinkler above the kitchen, a desperate play to possibly create chaotic openings and get Lonestar attention on the scene before they were all bagged and tagged.
