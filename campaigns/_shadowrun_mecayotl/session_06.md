# A Familiar Face
> Saturday, February 9, 2086

As **Neon Ghost** and **Toolbox** ghosted from the warehouse, their commlinks pinged with a dataline from **Bull**, right before they locked optics on a familiar chummer: **Wyrm**. After some quick meet-and-greets, the runners bugged out to *FF*.

**Wyrm** ran some legwork on the manor hosting the shindig, scoping the astral space, but turned up null on the mojo. Meanwhile, **NEEV** pinged a suit contact over at Ares, looking to snag a golden ticket to the meat-meet. **Ana Lopez** was willing to slide **NEEV** the passes, but demanded some paydata dirt on the new ride in exchange. 


> Thursday, February 14, 2086

**Toolbox**, **Getafix**, and **Sleeper** wrenched the *Bulldog* into a proper utility rig and tried to sniff out the target wheels. The recon was a bust; they couldn't get a solid trace on it.

# Flawless Execution
> Friday, February 15, 2086

## Scoping the Joint
**NEEV** waltzed into the gala with **Neon Ghost** and **Getafix** playing the heavy muscle. The meatspace was buzzing—wall-to-wall suits, heavy beats, and free-flowing soykaf and booze. The target was parked literally center stage. **Neon Ghost** scanned the astral for any wiz-drek that might brick their *Port Parasite*, but the magical background was clean. **NEEV** jacked in and got to hacking, only to hit a wall of local signal jammers. It took a few extra cycles, but she sliced through the noise and established a link.

## Boom
**Julian** swaggered out to schmooze the suits, then launched into his corporate pitch about the ride, rattling off the specs and blowing smoke about its top-tier tech. They ran a live-fire demo, lighting the drek out of it, and the armor didn't take a single scratch. Looked like it could frag up a pack of street-level newbies, but it'd crumple against prime runners, nott that the corp was advertising that little detail.

The crew chilled until the absolute perfect microsecond, then executed the databomb. It was total chaos: doors popped open, horns blared like banshees, a localized blast rocked the chassis, and rogue AR feeds flooded the windshield and displays. At first, the suits couldn't slice through the static, but then the trid-loop became clear. "Paying child support for 158 kids", "16,000 children, hundreds of fertility clinics", "Julian = Genghis Khan?", "Ares CEO kids not his own?", internal earning reports in the red, etc. It was an endless reel of simulated attacks exposing the ride's gaping security holes. Mr. Johnson actually showed up in the meatspace to witness the fireworks, and the crew caught him practically hacking up a lung laughing at the drek-show.

The new wheels were officially bricked on the PR front, and Ares' VP of Global Marketing looked like a slotting wannabe Genghis Khan.

The crew rolled back to *FF* to drain some synthehol and watch feeds blow up with the fallout gossip. A fresh face strolled into the joint and made a beeline straight for the runners. He tossed a certified credstick into **Neon Ghost's** hand, passing on the word that Mr. Johnson was fraggin' ecstatic.
