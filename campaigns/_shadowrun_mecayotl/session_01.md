# The Interrogation and Tail

## Sunday, January 12, 2086
Bigoonia tried to lean on the target to get her talking, but she wasn't buying what Bigoonia was selling. Gwendolyn bolted, cutting down a different alley to put some distance between them. She didn't notice Neon Ghost slipping into her shadow.

He tracked her all the way to her gym. To get inside, Ghost put on a face and convinced the desk clerk he was just there for a free trial membership. Once past the front desk, he covertly moved through the facility to run reconnaissance and keep eyes on her. He caught her spilling her guts to someone about a "big mother fucker." She was rattling, terrified, and asking for help. Once she finished working out, she packed her gear and headed back to her nest.


# Digging in Deeper
The crew decided it was time to peel back the layers on her personal life. A data dive pulled up Matrix-scraped photos of her husband, Reen, and their kid, Gesa. NEEV pulled the building's architectural schematics from the local grid, mapping out the structural entry points and pinning their apartment right at Unit 404.

NEEV and Sleeper ran a joint ops Matrix assault on Gwendolyn’s personal computer. They expected a walk in the park, but instead they slammed face-first into heavy resistance. It was serious cybernetic muscle, way too sophisticated for a standard Adept's personal tech. They traded code, burning through firewalls until they finally forced admin access. But before they could download the paydata, the host icon went completely dark. Someone on the physical side had literally pulled the power cord from the wall.

# What's a Little B&E?

## Monday, January 13, 2086
The team ran a stakeout until the family cleared out for the day for work and school. Time for some breaking and entering. Neon Ghost dropped down from the roof line while Bigoonia and Toolbox breached the side stairwell using a passkey. They linked up in the corridor and slipped inside Unit 404 without tripping any local alarms.

Neon Ghost located the dead computer, shoved the power jack back into the grid, and gave the signal. Sleeper and NEEV dived back into the matrix. This time they easily gained access. Sleeper stripped the drive clean, copied every scrap of data, and then completely formatted the system to leave nothing behind.

While the decker and technomancer were melting circuits, Bigoonia and Toolbox tossed the apartment. They pocketed a stray hard drive, some cheap jewelry, a Jazz inhaler, and a nice bottle of booze. The setup was intentional. Minus the wiped computer, the place looked like it got cleared out by a common street thief.
