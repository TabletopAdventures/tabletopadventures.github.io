# Diving In
> Friday, February 8, 2086

## Eyes On Target
The crew chewed the fat on their options after having to bug out. Everyone was sweating that the target might bounce, so **Toolbox** stayed on point, keeping an eye on the logistics center.

**Bigoonia** had to ghost the run, some go-gang drek came calling and she had to handle it.

## Chasing Poison Chrome
**Neon Ghost** and **Neev** hit up the filter shop and ran into **Screamin' Adler**. He took a real shine to **Neev**, and after some heavy haggling, he agreed to part with the *Port Parasite* for ¥5,000.

## Keynote Speaker
The crew managed to pin down the digs of Julian Montgomery, Ares VP of Global Marketing and the designated keynote speaker for the big unveiling.

The crew was angling for some simple infidelity, the kind of dirt that makes a corporate suit sweat. Their intel showed Julian was hitting events with a different woman on each arm. Turns out he didn't even have a spouse; on the surface, Julian was squeaky clean.

Unsatisfied, a small squad breached his place looking for dirt, and frag, did they hit the jackpot. The guy had a floor-to-ceiling bookshelf packed with photos of himself with kids of every age, from newborns to college grads. After digging into the data, the crew found he'd been greasing the palms of fertility clinics to impregnate every attractive woman that wanted a baby. 

The paper trail was staggering. They uncovered hundreds of paid-off clinics and estimated the man had at least 16,000 kids. Julian was shelling out roughly ¥500,000 a month, a cool six million nuyen a year, in child support to the women who actually knew he was the father. Julian fancies himself some kind of modern Genghis Khan. Blegh.
